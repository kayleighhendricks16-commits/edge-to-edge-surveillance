How does CCTV work?

A CCTV system uses cameras, built-in lenses, recording devices, monitors and network infrastructure to capture and transmit continuous video footage. Depending on your setup, footage is sent via cable or wirelessly to a recorder and then viewed live or played back when needed. Systems are scalable and can include features such as zoom, pan-tilt control, night vision, thermal imaging, audio, sirens and number plate recognition.

Advantages of CCTV

  • Cost-effective long-term site protection
  • Peace of mind for owners and managers
  • Possible reduction in insurance risk profile
  • Deterrence and crime prevention support
  • Remote monitoring where required
  • Strong integration with intruder alarm systems

Types of CCTV Cameras

Network Camera

Widely used for smart monitoring applications, including entrances, retail and public areas, with options such as facial recognition and extended network reach.

Thermal Camera

Used for perimeter protection, critical infrastructure and overheating detection, with thermal-only and thermal-plus-visible options available.

HDCVI Camera

Ideal when using existing analogue infrastructure while still benefiting from newer features such as higher resolution and enhanced transmission options.

PTZ Camera

Pan, tilt and zoom functionality for broad-area visibility and detail capture, with specialist variants available for demanding environments.

What is

CCTV Control Room

The CCTV Control Room provides a comprehensive monitoring and recording service using modern digital CCTV technology. Our trained operators monitor your premises 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to maintain constant visibility.

We can monitor multiple sites simultaneously and use advanced software to identify suspicious behaviour or potential risk indicators. Operators are positioned to react immediately to security incidents and emergency events.

Edge to Edge is committed to reliable, efficient service and responsive client support, with a strong focus on keeping your people, property and operations protected.

How does the CCTV control room work?

A CCTV Control Room combines camera networks, display monitors and secure recording systems into one central monitoring environment. Trained personnel oversee live feeds, review events, and respond to alarms or security triggers as they happen.

Footage is recorded and stored securely for retrieval during investigations, incident follow-up or compliance requirements. The system can also track movement at key zones such as entrances, exits and vehicle routes, improving situational awareness across your site.

Advantages of a CCTV control room

  • Provides a central point to monitor all CCTV footage.
  • Supports real-time detection and deterrence of suspicious activity.
  • Enables quick and effective response to security incidents.
  • Allows multiple sites or areas to be monitored at once.
  • Supports investigation and review of previous incidents.
  • Helps monitor staff activity and compliance with security protocols.
