Established in 2001, Edge to Edge has grown into one of South Africa’s main providers of CCTV and integrated security solutions, serving clients across Gauteng and KZN.

Edge to Edge

Client-focused security solutions built on long-term trust

With a wide variety of clients including retail premises, educational institutions, corporate offices, hospitals and larger industrial sites, Edge to Edge delivers solutions that improve both security and productivity. We are known for high service standards, reliable support, and up-to-date technology.

Proof of our service is reflected in longstanding corporate client relationships. Our branches include fully equipped workshops that service electronic equipment on-site and off-site. We also supply rental options for electronic security equipment, with optional ownership agreements after contract maturity.

Edge to Edge has undergone re-engineering to incorporate Black Economic Empowerment (BEE) principles and has advanced into Biometric and IP technologies to remain ahead in a fast-changing electronic security industry.
