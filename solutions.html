Finance Options

We give you finance options

An affordable way to finance non-income generating capital equipment. To help you decide which option best suits your needs, we have summarised the key advantages of the rental model.

Request a Quote

Advantages

  • Monthly rentals make capital equipment more affordable by avoiding large upfront outlay.
  • Rentals are generally 100% tax deductible as an operating expense, helping reduce income tax obligations.
  • Upfront VAT payment is avoided; customers can claim VAT through monthly payments made.
  • As an operating expense, rentals are not managed through capital expenditure budgets.
  • Off-balance-sheet treatment can support improved current and return-on-asset ratios while reducing administration tied to asset registers and depreciation schedules.
  • Equipment can be upgraded before contract end, allowing flexibility to align with newer technology.
  • Annual rental escalation can be linked to inflation to reduce initial costs.
  • Useful for foreign-owned companies because rentals are recorded as operating expense rather than current liability.
