About

Your Security is Our Business

Closed circuit television (CCTV), like all electronics, is constantly evolving in both application and functionality. Edge to Edge has product specialists who continuously assess market developments, evaluate practical use cases, and approve only the products that meet strict quality, reliability and support standards.

Effective system design requires more than equipment selection. Edge to Edge’s trained team develops bespoke solutions that strengthen security while also supporting business operations such as stock control and standard operating procedures.

CCTV solutions generally fall into three main options: Analogue, High-Definition Analogue and IP (Internet Protocol). Whether integrating into existing infrastructure or planning a new deployment, Edge to Edge can design the right solution for your requirements.

FAQ

Questions businesses often ask

Clear answers for planning CCTV, access control, alarms, perimeter protection and support with Edge to Edge.

What types of businesses does Edge to Edge support?

Edge to Edge works with commercial, industrial, retail, healthcare, education, logistics and corporate environments that need practical security systems and dependable support.

Can you design a system around an existing site?

Yes. Existing infrastructure, site risks, access points and operational needs can be reviewed before recommending CCTV, access control, alarms, boom gates or perimeter solutions.

Do you provide maintenance and repairs?

Yes. Edge to Edge provides maintenance and repair support to help keep security equipment operational, reduce downtime and support day-to-day business continuity.

Are finance and SLA options available?

Yes. Finance options are available for non-income generating capital equipment, and Service Level Agreement options help keep systems maintained and supported.

Which areas do you service?

Edge to Edge has branches in Gauteng and KZN, with experience supporting businesses across Johannesburg, Durban and wider regional or multi-site operations.
